# EXP-0069 — where kata stands against llama.cpp CPU, per context length

**Status:** PASSED (measurement)
**Purpose:** replace the single-length 453-vs-443 comparison with a curve, since
the prefill gap is a function of context length and the earlier reports compared
different lengths against each other.

## Method

Same model `LFM2.5-24B-A2B-Q4_K_M.gguf`, 16 threads, GPU hidden for both engines
(`HIP_VISIBLE_DEVICES="" ROCR_VISIBLE_DEVICES=""`). Three runs per kata point,
two per llama.cpp point (`llama-bench -r 2`). Prompts are prefixes/repeats of the
same 559-token text, so token distribution is identical across lengths.

## Prefill

| tokens | kata | llama.cpp CPU | ratio |
|---|---|---|---|
| 559 | 515.5 / 518.5 / 525.0 | 448.70 ± 0.75 | **1.15x** |
| 2236 | 589.2 / 584.8 / 595.3 | 434.00 ± 3.16 | **1.35x** |
| 8944 | 525.8 / 529.0 / 525.5 | 395.72 ± 0.53 | **1.33x** |
| 16770 | 430.5 / 431.8 / 430.5 | 352.12 ± 0.55 | **1.22x** |

kata is ahead at every length, peak advantage at 2-9k, and the lead narrows as
the quadratic attention term grows — at 16k, 1.22x.

## Decode

| | kata | llama.cpp CPU | ratio |
|---|---|---|---|
| ms/token | 22.38-22.43 | — | — |
| tok/s | 43.94 | 44.79 ± 0.07 | 0.98x |

Decode is at parity, inside 2%. Budget: `ffn 14.09 ms (63%) | mix 5.45 (24%) |
lm 2.81 (13%) | norm 0.02`, against a 1529 MB/token traffic floor of 19.6 ms at
78 GB/s — i.e. 88% of the floor.

## Correction to the traffic ceiling

The 78 GB/s figure used in earlier experiments came from a harness whose working
set was partly L3-resident. Re-measured properly (`/tmp/peakbw.cpp`,
`/tmp/peakbw2.cpp`, 4 GiB buffer, 16 independent bands):

| threads | GB/s |
|---|---|
| 1 | 62.0 |
| 4 | 62.7 |
| 8 | 63.0 |
| 16 | **73.5** |
| 32 | 72.5 |

So the real DRAM read ceiling is **73.5 GB/s**, and decode at 22.4 ms/token
moves 1529 MB/token at **68.3 GB/s = 93% of the ceiling**. That is the honest
bound: decode has ~7% left, not 12%.
