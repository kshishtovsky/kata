# EXP-0067 — int8 score pass: measured, 0.70x, REJECTED

**Status:** FAILED (not adopted)
**Hypothesis:** positions-in-lanes int8 QK through `vpdpbusd` retires 4 MAC per
lane-slot instead of 1 FMA per lane-slot, so the score pass should approach the
311 GMAC/s int8 peak instead of the 80.5 GMAC/s fp32 peak — a 3.9x kernel win.

## Probe

Same 64 query rows x npos positions x 4 query heads sharing one kv head, both
arms computing the identical score matrix, every output sunk through a
`volatile` accumulator, samples printed, MAC counts asserted equal
(`/tmp/i8qk7.cpp`):

| npos | harness fp32 | int8 RP=1 | RP=2 | RP=4 | RP=8 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 32768 | 6.76-8.10 ms | 28.32 ms | 13.58 | **9.79** | 11.07 |
| GMAC/s | 66-79 | 19.0 | 39.5 | **54.9** | 48.5 |
| vs fp32 | 1.00x | 0.29x | 0.57x | **0.70x** | 0.61x |
| % of int8 peak | — | 6% | 13% | **18%** | 16% |

int8 score error after zero-point correction: **rel 1.69e-02** — acceptable for
scores (softmax then sees a 1.7% perturbation), so the rejection is purely about
speed.

## Why it loses

`vpdpbusd` needs **src1 unsigned**. Our weights are unsigned in the VNNI GEMM,
but a score dot has q and k both signed, so one of them must be stored as
signed+128 and corrected with `-128 * sum(k)` per 4-dim group. That correction
is a full extra float pass over 16 positions per tile — and it recurs exactly as
often as the dot itself. Net effect: the 4x MAC density is consumed by the
correction plus two `vcvtepi32_ps` + two `vmulps` per tile.

ILP is required to get anywhere (RP=1 → 0.29x, RP=4 → 0.70x) because four
accumulator chains cannot cover the 5-cycle `vpdpbusd` latency, and RP=8 loses
again to register pressure.

## The number that stops this line of work

The **fp32** arm of the same harness reaches **66-79 GMAC/s = 82-99% of the
80.5 GMAC/s fp32 core peak** when the K row is L3-resident. An int8 kernel
capped at 18% of its own peak cannot beat a float kernel already at 99% of its
peak, no matter how the bytes are laid out.

Corollary worth recording: the in-engine score pass measures **52 GMAC/s** in the
QK-only ablation (EXP-0066) versus 79 GMAC/s here, i.e. the engine loses ~35%
somewhere between the isolated shape and `prefill_group4`. That gap is float
work, not quantisation work, and it is the only remaining lossless lever on this
stage.

## Decision

No int8 or int4 score pass. Recorded as NEEDS_RESEARCH only if someone finds a
zero-point scheme that costs fewer than ~2 ops per 16 MAC (e.g. asymmetric k
with an unsigned k and signed q, which `vpdpbusd` also supports but then k must
be re-quantised per position at write time).
