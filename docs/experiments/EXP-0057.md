# EXP-0057 — QK is within 1.6x of the lossless ceiling; the 18% figure was a unit error

Status: PASSED (measurement), REJECTED (all three optimisations)
Date: 2026-11-27

## Why this ran

EXP-0056 left one open question: attention core is 42.7% of a 32k prefill and QK
is 76% of the core, so what is the actual lossless ceiling on QK? The log said
"QK runs at 50 GMAC/s = 30% of fp32 peak" and, later, "18% of fp32 peak". Both
numbers were used to justify a 3.3-5.5x target.

## The unit error

`tools/peak_bench.cpp` freezes fp32 at **160.9 GFLOP/s per core**. One FMA is two
flops, so the MAC ceiling is **80.5 GMAC/s**, not 160.9. QK measured 50 GMAC/s
against 80.5 is **62% of peak, not 30%**, and the lossless ceiling on the whole
score pass is **1.61x, not 3.3-5.5x**.

Corrected instruction budget per position (4 heads x 64 dim = 256 MAC): 16 zmm
FMAs, double-pumped on Zen 4 = 32 x 256-bit ops = 16 cycles of FMA issue, so 256
MAC / 16 cycles = 16 MAC/cycle = 80 GMAC/s at 5.0 GHz. Measured 43-50 GMAC/s is
54-63% of that, which is a normal figure for a loop with a horizontal reduce.

Patched in `docs/experiments/EXPERIMENT_LOG.md` (EXP-0054 "What this changes"),
and the scatter claim in the same paragraph was also corrected against EXP-0055.

## Three optimisations measured, all rejected

`/tmp/qk2.cpp`, single core, `taskset -c 0`, 20 reps, 32k positions, engine shape
(4 heads per kv head, head_dim 64):

| variant | 32k | GMAC/s |
|---|---|---|
| shipped: q reloaded from L1 per position, 4 separate `_mm512_reduce_add_ps` | 4.9 ms | 34-43 |
| q hoisted into 16 zmm, k as an FMA memory operand, one shared 4-way hadd | 4.8 ms | 35-43 |

**1.03x** — the q reloads are free (L1, 4 loads/2 fma_ports), and the shared hadd
saves nothing. Numerically identical to 1.9e-06.

`/tmp/qk3.cpp`, independent-chain count per head:

| chains | 32k | GMAC/s |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 3.8 ms | 44 |
| 2 | 3.9 ms | 43 |
| 4 | 4.5 ms | 37 |
| heads halved to 2 (ch 2) | 2.7 ms | 31 corrected |

More chains lose because the accumulator count goes 16 -> 32 and spills; halving
the heads loses because the 4-head reduction amortises over fewer FMAs. **There
is no ILP left to find.**

A harness bug was caught here and is why the 2-head arm is listed as "corrected":
`mac = npos*4*HD*reps` is hardcoded to 4 heads, so the 2-head arm reported
62 GMAC/s when it does half the MACs — 31 GMAC/s, i.e. worse, not better. Third
time this project has reported a speedup that was an artefact of the oracle
rather than the kernel.

## What is left on the score pass

1.6x at most, and it requires fewer instructions per MAC. Of those:

- int8 `vpdpbusd` QK: 311 vs 165 GMAC/s per core on paper (1.88x) against a QK
  already at 62% of the lossless peak — so a realistic 1.3-1.4x on QK, i.e. ~1.15x
  on the core and ~1.06x on a 32k prefill, and it changes numerics.
- PV fusion into the QK pass: removes the second walk over V, but PV is only
  0.72 ms of the 2.4 ms core step measured in EXP-0056, and fusing it needs P
  held across the position walk — a 64 KB working set per row.
- Nothing else: unroll, prefetch, hugepages, chain count, q hoisting and reduce
  sharing are all measured negative.

## Conclusion

The attention core is not the place to spend the next iteration. At 42.7% of a
32k run and 1.6x of lossless headroom behind it, the whole stage is worth at most
1.28x end-to-end, and every lossless lever on it has now been measured and
rejected. The MoE GEMM (47% of VNNI peak at n=1536) and the FFN glue are larger
absolute targets in the same run.

## Rule adopted

Every "X% of peak" must state its unit on both sides. `docs/experiments` now has
one such error; `tools/peak_bench.cpp` freezes both GFLOP/s and GMAC/s so the
next comparison cannot silently mix them.
